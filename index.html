About

I like making computers notice people.

I study applied computer science at HTW Berlin, and most of what I have built sits somewhere between a camera and a database. My last project read body positions and gestures from a webcam; my internship before that was the other end of the same problem — containers, reverse proxies and CI, so that something built in a seminar can actually survive being deployed. I am drawn to digital assistance systems and to the point where a person and a machine have to understand each other.

What I have built so far

Team project HTW Berlin, 2026

Body Explorer

A digital learning and assistance system: a web prototype that recognises body parts and gestures through an ordinary webcam. I worked on the layered architecture that carries camera input through perception and interpretation up to the interface — the interesting part was deciding what counts as a gesture and what is just a person moving. Organised in the team with GitHub and Kanban, where I also worked on integration and project management.

JavaScript, HTML/CSS, MediaPipe Pose, Face and Hands, Webcam APIs.
